MTG Collection

Share a decklist or a collection export from any Android app and it goes straight into the collection.

Download the app

Always the latest build. Not on the Play Store, and not going to be.

Installing

  1. Open this page on the phone and tap Download.
  2. Open the downloaded file. Android asks once whether to allow installs from your browser — say yes.
  3. Open ManaBox, export, Share, pick MTG Collection.

The first share asks for the password, once. After that it remembers, and the password itself is never stored.

Getting updates

Android will not tell you about new versions of an app it did not install. Obtainium will: install it, add this as an app, and it watches for releases and updates on its own.

https://github.com/mattshoe/mtg-api

Or just come back here and download it again — installing over the top keeps your token and settings.

Why an app at all

The web app is on the phone too, and every other part of it works there. Receiving a shared file is the one thing it cannot do: Chrome matched every share and then handed over an empty body, because reading another app's file on a web page's behalf is a line it will not cross. A real app holds that permission itself.
