Cards and decks now belong to your account itself, not to a name that has to match. A new account starts empty. Renaming yourself never moves your cards.

Decks have their own random link that never changes, even when you rename the deck. Two people can each have a deck called Milly Moth. Old deck links stop working.

Scripts that send `"owner":"matt"` to add or remove cards now get told to leave it out, or to name the collection by its key.
