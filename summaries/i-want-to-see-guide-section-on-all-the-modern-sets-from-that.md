What was built: A Sets tile on the Guide's front page, right after Lore. It opens a page of tiles, one per set, oldest first: Phyrexia All Will Be One, March of the Machine with The Aftermath, Wilds of Eldraine, The Lost Caverns of Ixalan, Murders at Karlov Manor, Outlaws of Thunder Junction, Bloomburrow, Duskmourn, Aetherdrift, Tarkir Dragonstorm, Edge of Eternities and Lorwyn Eclipsed. Each set is its own page with 6 to 15 tabs picked for that set, the world, the story in its arcs, the factions, the people, the mechanics, the cards worth knowing and the Commander decks, between about 7,000 and 17,000 words each. They work like Lore pages, links to Lore articles and character profiles, card names marked, Read on through each set and into the next, back goes to Sets, and the Guide search finds them.
Choices: Read "the modern sets from that few years" as the Magic story sets from early 2023 to now. Universes Beyond sets like Final Fantasy, Spider-Man and Avatar were left out, since they are not Magic's own story. Foundations and Modern Horizons 3 were left out too, they have no main story. Each set was written by its own agent and checked against the official Magic Story episodes, MTG Wiki and Scryfall, every card name included.
Liberties: The writers flagged a few details that rest on MTG Wiki alone or on one card's flavor text, and a few soft opinions in the cards tabs, like which cards saw competitive play. The story tabs for sets that already had a Lore article retell that article in more detail. Sets after Lorwyn Eclipsed are not included.
