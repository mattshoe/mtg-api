What was built: A search bar at the top of the Guide, phone and website. While something is typed, the tiles are replaced by a count and a card for every article that has every word in it, saying which section it is from, its title, and the bit of text around the word. Tapping a card opens that article on its own tab. Back to the Guide keeps the search and its results. A word nothing has says so, and clearing the bar brings the tiles back.
Choices: An article is one of Phyrexia's eras or one of the story's articles, the same things the Guide shows as tabs. Every word typed has to be in the article, in any capitals, so a second word narrows it. Results come in the order the Guide tells them. It searches what you read, so link and card markup is not matched, and a word inside a longer one counts (war finds warrior).
Liberties: The colour combinations, the colour wheel and the rules are not searched here. They are reference pages rather than articles, and the rules already have their own search.
