What was wrong: On a card's page, In decks listed every deck in the database that had the card, whoever built it, so your Sol Ring showed Kayla's decks next to yours.
Why: The query behind In decks had no owner at all. The Decks page was scoped to your collection back when Kayla's decks first showed up there, but the card page's deck list was missed, and two old tests even pinned it as unscoped.
Fix: In decks now asks for the decks of the collection on screen, the same way the Decks page does. Your own collection when signed in, the one in the address when you open somebody's link, every collection only for a visitor who is not signed in, and nothing at all in the moment before the app knows who you are. Printings on the card page are unchanged. Same shared code on the phone and the website, with a test on each driving the real app.
