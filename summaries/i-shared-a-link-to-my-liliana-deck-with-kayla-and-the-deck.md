What was wrong: A link to your Liliana deck opened on Kayla's phone and sat on "Loading the deck..." forever. On the website the same link showed her own decks instead of yours.
Why: The deck page looked for the deck's name, commander and owner only among the decks of the collection on screen, which for Kayla is her own. Your deck is never there, so the cards loaded and the page never had a deck to show them under.
Fix: When the deck is not on that shelf, the app now asks for it by its key, whoever owns it, and opens it. A key that names no deck now says "there is no deck at that address" instead of spinning, and a failed load says why and can be pulled to try again. Same change on the phone and the website. Also, once it opened, Kayla would have been offered Edit list, Rename and Disassemble on your deck, which the server refuses. Those now follow the deck's owner, not the collection on screen.
