What was built: A Brackets tile in the Guide, right after Rules. It opens one page with a card per bracket, Exhibition, Core, Upgraded, Optimized and cEDH, lowest first. Each card shows a big number, the name and one line, and is shut until tapped. Open, it says what the bracket is, how long games tend to run, and five rules. Phone and website.
Choices: Every bracket answers the same five questions in the same order, Game Changers, two-card combos, mass land denial, extra turns and tutors, so two open cards compare line for line. Each answer is a mark and a short word (a cross for none, a tilde for limited, a tick for anything), told apart by shape rather than colour. More than one bracket can be open at once. The tile sits between Rules and Game Changers, since Game Changers only mean something once you know the brackets.
Liberties: The bracket rules are written into the app from Wizards' published brackets as I know them, so they change when the app does, not when Wizards announce. The turn counts and the bracket 3 combo rule (late game only) come from Wizards' later update and are worth a glance against the current article. No search on this page.
