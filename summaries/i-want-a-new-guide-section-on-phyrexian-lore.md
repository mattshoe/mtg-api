What was built: A third Guide tile, Phyrexia, with its own page on the phone and the website. A row of tabs across the top, one per era, eight of them: The Thran, The Brothers' War, Urza's Long War, Rath, The Invasion, Mirrodin, New Phyrexia and March of the Machine. Each tab is a chapter of four to six paragraphs told as a story, with a title, and four of them close on a line of flavour text from a card. A Read on button at the foot of each chapter goes to the next era. The open tab is part of the address, so a bookmark lands on that era and back goes to the Guide tiles.
Choices: Set in a serif face at a reading size with roomy lines, in one centred column no wider than a page of a book, so a tablet does not stretch the lines out. On a phone the tabs are one row that scrolls sideways rather than wrapping. The open tab is told apart by brightness and an underline, not colour. All the words live in one place and both apps draw from it.
Liberties: The story is my own retelling, light on names and with no dates, as asked. I only quoted flavour text I was confident of: Phyrexian Arena, Mirran Crusader, Elesh Norn, Grand Cenobite, and the creed All will be one. The last era ends just after the March of the Machine, since that is where the Phyrexian story stops for now. Some details are softened rather than risk getting them wrong, such as exactly how Urza died.
