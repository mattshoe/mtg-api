What was built: Under the card name on the carousel, a line reading type, then the colour identity as mana symbols, then rarity, separated by dots. Phone and website, deck and Library carousels both.
Choices: Type is the full type line, so subtypes like Elf Druid stay visible. The old type line further down was removed since this line now carries it. A colourless card shows the colourless symbol rather than nothing.
Liberties: A card nobody owns has no type, identity or rarity to read, so the line is left out for it rather than showing empty dots.
