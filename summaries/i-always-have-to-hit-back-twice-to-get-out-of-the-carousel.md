What was wrong: On the phone, back had to be pressed twice to close the carousel after typing a card name in the Library. The first press did nothing you could see.
Why: Back closes the Library's card-name suggestion list before anything else. Nothing put that list away when the carousel opened over it, or when you left the Library, so it stayed open behind the carousel or on screens that never draw it. The first press closed that list, the second closed the carousel.
Fix: Opening the carousel, or any other overlay, now closes the suggestion list, and so does leaving the screen. What you typed stays in the box. The rule is in the shared core, so the website gets it too. The website never had the double press, because its back skips the list, but the list was left open under the carousel there as well.
