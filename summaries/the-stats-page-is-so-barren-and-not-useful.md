What was built: The Stats page keeps its totals and adds a tab for every card type you own, with Everything as the landing tab. Each tab shows six headline figures and up to thirteen charts: colour and exact colour identity rings, card types, mana curve, rarity, top 15 creature types, copies by price band, value by colour, value by rarity, top 10 sets, cards by release year, top 10 artists and the 10 most valuable cards. Colour pips and rarity chips filter everything on top of the tab. Phone and website.
Choices: The charts are the deck page's own bars, rings and curve, so they look the same. Every number is worked out on the device from one query of your whole collection, so switching tabs or filters is instant and makes no new request. A colour filter keeps cards whose identity has any of the picked colours, and colourless is a colour you can pick. Tabs stay put when you filter, they just show 0, so a tab never moves out from under your thumb. Charts sit side by side on a tablet or an opened folding phone.
Liberties: The price, release year, artist and most valuable charts were not asked for by name. Counts are by copies, the same as the deck page. Value charts are in whole dollars.
