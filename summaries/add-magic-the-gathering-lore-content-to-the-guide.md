What was built: A fifth Guide tile, The story of Magic, on the phone and the website. The front page lists the ten sections in your order, each with a one sentence hook and a button per article, 51 articles in all. An article opens with its section and title, a row of tabs picked for that story in the order it happens, the narration, a Read on button to the next tab and at the end of the article to the next article, and a Where it is told list of sets, novels, Magic Story articles and the MTG Wiki pages it was checked against. Links in the text go to the other articles, including every pair you named. Thirteen people have profiles: Urza, Yawgmoth, Karn, Sorin, Nicol Bolas, Ugin, Nahiri, Elesh Norn, Jace, Teferi, Liliana, Gideon and Chandra. Every article that names one of them links to their profile, and the profile's Appears in list is worked out by reading the articles, so it cannot go stale.
Choices: The article, tab and profile are all in the address, so a bookmark and back work, and back from an article goes to the sections, then to the Guide. Links are underlined and card names are in italics rather than coloured. The text sits in one centred reading column on a tablet. Narration has no dialogue at all, a quote sits in its own block under the tab with the card it comes from, and every quote is flavour text checked word for word on Scryfall. Every card name was checked on Scryfall too. Retcons and conflicting sources get one sentence in the narration with the current version. The old Phyrexia tile is left as it was.
Liberties: About 75,000 words, researched against MTG Wiki and the official Magic Story episodes and written by six writer agents, one or two sections each, then held to the rules by tests. I have not read every word. The Omenpath Era sets lean on the Magic Story episodes, since the wiki is thin there.
Left out because it could not be verified: the Sylex inscription's exact wording, whether Kayla slept with Mishra at Kroog, how long Tawnos was in the coffin, Harbin's later fate, Freyalise and Jodah in the World Spell, Gamalgoth, Rath's true origin and date, who named Memnarch, Urabrask's fate, the details of the Meditation Realm duel and the old Elder Dragon War, Bolas's citadel, which planeswalkers kept their sparks beyond the ones the wiki confirms, the plane Sorin and Nahiri watched Ulamog eat, Gideon at Fort Keff, whether Liliana swore the oath, how Sorin got out of Nahiri's stone, Avacyn's own words, the Sinpurged, whether Griselbrand is gone for good, why Ravnica was sealed before the Guildpact, how Melek left the maze race, whether the Omenpaths caused the Desparkening, what became of Realmbreaker, Akul's fate and home plane, the Bloomburrow names that are only in the Planeswalker's Guide, the Duskmourn cult leader's name and Marina's later bargain, final Aetherdrift placings, the blue cloak at the end of Tarkir Dragonstorm and whether Jace lived, the Edge of Eternities android's name, and any tie between the eld and the Eldrazi. All dates were left out on purpose.
