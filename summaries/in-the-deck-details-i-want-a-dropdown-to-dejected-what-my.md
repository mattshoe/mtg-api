What was built: A dropdown on the deck page's mana curve. All cards is the default and draws the one curve it always did. Card type draws a curve per type, Colour a curve per colour. Phone and website.
Choices: Every graph shares one scale, so a column of three cards is the same height in every graph. Lands stay out, as they do in the main curve. The commander counts under its own type, usually Creatures, rather than a Commander graph of one card. A gold card counts once in each of its colours, the way a Human Warrior counts once as each type. Colourless comes last.
Liberties: A card's colour is read from its mana cost, and a card with no cost, like Ancestral Vision, takes its colour identity. The split graphs are shorter than the main one so a deck's worth fits on a phone. The choice resets to All cards when you leave the deck.
