What was wrong: Cracker Barrel listed Aang, Swift Savior and Witch Enchanter by their front face. Both did get added to your collection, but the deck still treated them as cards you do not have, so it looked like nothing was added. The other seven new cards were fine.
Why: The collection stores a two-faced card under both faces, "Aang, Swift Savior // Aang and La, Ocean's Fury". Saving a deck looked cards up by the exact name typed, so a front face matched nothing. It bought the card, which comes back with the full name, then saved the deck row with the short one, and the two never joined. A front face of a card you already owned got bought a second time the same way.
Fix: Saving a deck list, new or edited, now turns a front face into the full name before it checks what you own, and again after it adds anything. Server only, so the phone and the website both get it from the same save.
