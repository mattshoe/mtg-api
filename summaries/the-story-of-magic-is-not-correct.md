What was wrong: The story of Magic was one Guide tile. It opened on a page listing all ten sections, and each article opened on its own with a second set of tabs, so it read as one big article rather than ten.
Why: It was built that way, as sections of articles of tabs under a single tile, instead of each section being a page of its own the way Phyrexia is.
Fix: The single tile is gone. Each of the ten sections is its own Guide tile after Phyrexia, with its section hook as the blurb, and opens like Phyrexia: a row of tabs, one per article, at #/guide/<section>/<article>, with Guide back above it. The parts that used to be an article's own tabs are now told one after another in that tab, each under its own small heading, so no words were lost. Read on goes to the next article and, at the end of a section, to the next section. A link to an article in another section opens that section. A profile opens inside the section you were reading, with a button back up to it. Phone and website both.
