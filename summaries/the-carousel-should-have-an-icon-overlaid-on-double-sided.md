What was built: A round flip button in the top right corner of the card in the carousel, only on cards with a real printed back. Tap it and the card shows its back, tap it again and it shows the front. Works over a deck, the Library, a fetch list and Game Changers, where Tergrid is the one two-faced card. Phone and website, phone sized and tablet sized.
Choices: Two-faced means transforming cards, modal double-faced cards, reversible cards and double-faced tokens, the same set the is:dfc search uses. Split, adventure and flip cards print both halves on one side so they get no button, and meld cards don't either because their back is half of a different card. Swiping to the next card always lands on its front. The button stays put while you pinch zoom the art, and you can flip while zoomed.
Liberties: The Banned list has no two-faced card on it today, Erayo is a flip card with both halves on one side, so nothing there gets a button. The deck and fetch list queries now also fetch each card's layout.
