What was built: The Stats page is a real tabbed page. A tab bar along the top, Overview first and then a tab per card type you own, the open tab underlined. Under it the colour and rarity filters, the tab's headline numbers, then its charts in four headed sections, Colours, Make-up, Value and Printings. Phone and website.
Choices: The tabs are one line that scrolls sideways instead of a wrapped row of chips, so they no longer look like the filters under them. The Overview keeps every whole-collection chart and its numbers now include decks and free copies, so the separate eight-line totals list is gone. A type tab only shows what says something about that type, its colours, curve, rarity, prices, most valuable and top sets. Creature types moved to the Creatures tab. Card types, value by colour and rarity, release years and artists stay on the Overview only. Empty charts and empty sections are hidden.
Liberties: The landing tab is called Overview instead of Everything. The totals list still shows for a collection with no cards, where there is nothing to chart.
