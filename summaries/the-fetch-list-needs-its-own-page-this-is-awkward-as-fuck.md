What was built: Fetch list split into two pages. #/fetch is the entry box and the saved lists. Find cards goes to #/fetch/list, a page of its own that shows a loading view (the five mana symbols going round a ring, and how many cards it is looking for) and then the filed list. Every card on it has a box to tick, and the whole row is the box. A count at the top says how many are found. Edit at the top opens the list text, Find cards files it again and every tick stays. Back, or the Lists button, goes to the root. A failed Find says what went wrong on the list page with a Try again button.
Choices: Ticks are kept by card name, so an edit keeps the tick on any card still on the list. The list on its page is stored on the device with its ticks whether or not it was saved, and shows on the root as "Unsaved list" so it can be reached again. Ticking a saved list writes into the saved one, and editing a saved list updates it.
Liberties: Saving still needs a title, as before, and lives on the list page now. Missing cards have no box, since there is nothing to fetch. The entry box on the root starts empty after Find, so pasting a new list never touches the one on its page.
