What was wrong: A Fetch list saved on the website never showed on the phone, and the other way round.
Why: Saved lists were only ever kept on the device that saved them, in the browser's storage or the phone's.
Fix: They are kept against your account on the server now. Both apps read them when you sign in and send them on every save, delete and tick. Lists already saved on a device are added to your account the first time it syncs. If the lists cannot load or cannot save, a red message says so, the device keeps its copy, and opening Fetch tries again. Phone and website.
