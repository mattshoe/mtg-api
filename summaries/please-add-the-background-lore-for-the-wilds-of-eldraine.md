What was built: A Wilds of Eldraine article in the Omenpath Era, between The Omenpaths and Thunder Junction. Six tabs in story order. The Wicked Slumber covers the invasion reaching Eldraine and Talion and the three witches putting every Phyrexian to sleep, and the curse that never lifted. Talion's Bargain is Kellan taking the quest. Agatha's Cauldron covers meeting Ruby, Agatha in her own pot, and the beanstalk. The Frozen Heart is Hylda giving up her crown and the truth about Talion. The Ardent Queen is Rowan joining Eriette and Ashiok at Ardenvale. Broken Oaths is the fight for the castle, Kellan learning Oko is his father, and the Omenpath he walks through.
Choices: Built like every other story article, with links, card names, a sourced quote and sources at the end. The Omenpaths and Thunder Junction now link to it where they mention Eldraine, and it links on to both. Same on the phone and the website, since both draw it from shared code.
Liberties: Placed second in the era, since it is the first set of the Omenpath story. The text was checked against the MTG Wiki pages for the set, the Wicked Slumber, Kellan, Eriette and Rowan, not the original Magic Story episodes. Things the wiki was vague on, like what happened at Hylda's castle while Kellan slept, are left vague.
