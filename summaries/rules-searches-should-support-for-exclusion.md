What was built: On the rules page, on the phone and the website, a minus in front of a word leaves out every rule with that word, so trample -planeswalkers finds the trample rules except the planeswalker one. A minus in front of quotes leaves out every rule with that whole phrase, like -"combat damage". Both mix with normal words and quoted phrases.
Choices: The search lives in the shared code, so both apps read the same rule. Case does not matter, and a phone keyboard's curly quotes count, the same as the rest of the search.
Liberties: A search that only leaves things out, like -trample on its own, finds nothing, the same as a blank search, since it would otherwise list nearly the whole rulebook. A minus in front of a number belongs to the number, so -1/-1 counters is still searched for. A minus on its own or inside a word, like two-player, is just a hyphen.
