What was built: One Lore tile on the Guide's front page, after Colour combinations, The colour wheel, Rules and Game Changers. It opens a page of the twelve story tiles that used to sit on the front page: The Blind Eternities, Phyrexia's Origins, Phyrexia, Brothers' War, Weatherlight Saga, Planeswalkers, Ravnica, Nicol Bolas, Eldrazi and Zendikar, Innistrad, New Phyrexia, Omenpath Era. Inside a story the back button says Lore and goes there. Phone and website.
Choices: Each section sits where most of its story happens, since most span thousands of years. The Blind Eternities goes first as the setting everything floats in. Phyrexia's Origins goes before Phyrexia because Halcyon comes before the fall of the Thran. New Phyrexia goes late because Scars and the invasion are its bulk, even though Argentum was built just after Apocalypse. Every story keeps its old address, so bookmarks and links still work.
Liberties: A story opened from a search result goes back to the search, not to Lore, since the person never went through Lore to get there. Read on at the end of a section still follows the old section order, not the Lore order.
