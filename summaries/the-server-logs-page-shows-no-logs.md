What was wrong: Server Logs always said Nothing logged, on the phone and the website.
Why: Two things. Neither app ever asked the server for the log, the page had no loader at all and drew its empty state. And the server's /logs only accepted the old operator password token, which the apps never hold, so a signed-in admin would have been refused even if they had asked.
Fix: /logs and /logs/stats now let in an account with the admin role as well as the operator, and a signed-in account without the role gets a 403 saying it needs admin. Both apps fetch the log, info and above, newest first, when the page opens, on a second visit and on pull to refresh. A failure shows the server's reason on the page instead of a spinner or an empty log, and pulling or coming back asks again.
