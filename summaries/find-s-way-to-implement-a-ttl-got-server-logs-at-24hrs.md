What was built: Server logs now live 24 hours instead of 7 days. A new hourly Cloudflare cron trigger deletes every log row older than 24 hours and does nothing else. The daily 08:10 job still prunes too. Reading the log (GET /logs and /logs/stats, which is what the Server Logs page on the phone and the website calls) deletes old rows first, so the page can never show a row past the window, even in the minutes before the next hourly run. Stats cap their look-back at 24 hours and report retention_hours.
Choices: Hourly cron plus a prune on read, rather than a prune on every request. Pruning on every request would add a delete to every call the Worker serves. The hourly trigger runs only the log prune, not the whole daily job, so prices are not re-fetched 24 times a day.
Liberties: The old classic log page lost its 3 day and 7 day buttons, since there is nothing that old to show, and now says "kept 24 hours". No phone or website app change was needed: neither shows the retention or asks for more than 24 hours. The hourly trigger takes effect when the Worker deploys from this merge, and logs already older than a day are deleted on its first run or the first time the page is opened.
