What was wrong: The In a deck filter on the Library listed every deck in the database, whoever's cards were on screen, so your own Library offered Kayla's decks and hers offered yours.
Why: The list is read once when the app opens and it had no idea whose each deck was beyond a display name, so nothing could narrow it when the collection changed.
Fix: Each deck now carries its owner's public key, and the panel offers only the decks of the collection on screen. It changes the moment you move between collections, with no extra read. Phone and website.
Also: The owner in brackets is gone when the list is one person's, since it would say the same name on every row. A filter on one named deck is dropped when you move to another collection, because that collection's list does not have it. A visitor who has named nobody still sees everyone's decks with the owner in brackets, because they are looking at everyone's cards. The other lists in the panel, like sets and artists, still cover every collection, that was not what was asked.
