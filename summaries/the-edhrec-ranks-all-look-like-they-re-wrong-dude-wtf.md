What was wrong: Teysa Karlov showed as EDHREC #1,330 in the app, but EDHREC's commander page has her at #53 (58 today).
Why: Both numbers are right, they just measure different things. The app only had Scryfall's EDHREC rank, which ranks a card among every card. EDHREC's commander page ranks commanders among commanders. The app showed the first with nothing saying which one it was.
Fix: The Worker now asks EDHREC for a commander's rank among commanders. A commander's card page and its carousel show "Commander #58 on EDHREC", and the old number now reads "#1,330 of all cards". This works for legendary creatures, cards that say they can be your commander, and a deck's commander. Phone and website. The Library still sorts and filters by the all-cards rank, because the commander rank isn't stored.
