What was wrong: Find cards took minutes, and every card with more than one colour was lumped under Multicolor with the colours spelled out as words.
Why: The list waited on the Also check, about twenty-five Scryfall searches run one after another for a hundred-card deck, each a second or more. The collection itself answers in about a second. And the sections were keyed on a card's colours with a single bucket for anything over one.
Fix: The filing goes on screen as soon as the collection answers, with a line saying the Also check is still coming, and the Also check fills in when Scryfall finishes. Sections are keyed on colour identity, one per combination, headed by mana symbols in the order a card prints them, so Izzet is U then R and colourless is the C symbol.
