What was built: A second Guide tile, The colour wheel, with its own page on the phone and the website. Three short paragraphs say what the wheel is and what allies and enemies mean. Then the wheel itself, five mana symbols round a circle, white at the top going clockwise. Then a card per colour saying what it wants, what it is good at, what it is bad at, and naming its allies and enemies. Then the five allied pairs and five enemy pairs by guild name, each with a line on why they agree or clash.
Choices: Allies and enemies are worked out from the order round the wheel, not written down twice. Allies are joined by solid lines and enemies by dashed ones, so they are told apart by the dash rather than by colour. The wheel is a fixed size so it does not swell on a tablet, and the colour cards go two across once there is room. Pair names come from the same place the deck page gets them.
Liberties: The words about each colour are mine, written for someone new to it. The guild flavour in the enemy pairs (Orzhov as a church that sells forgiveness, Izzet as mad science) is a light touch, not a lore page.
