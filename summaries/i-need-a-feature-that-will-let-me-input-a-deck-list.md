What was built: Fetch, a new tab where Stats was. Paste a deck list, press Find cards, and every card comes back in one section per type, colour and rarity, in that order, with a section at the end for cards you do not own or own too few of. Each card can carry an Also check line naming the other rarities it could be filed under. A result can be saved under a title, which is required, and the saved lists sit at the bottom of the tab to open again or delete. Stats moved into the profile menu. Phone and website.
Choices: Called Fetch, like a fetchland fetching a card, because the bar wants one short word. Type is the one a sorted binder uses: a land is a land even if it is also a creature, otherwise a creature is a creature even if it is also an artifact or enchantment. Colour is the card's own colour, with Multicolor and Colorless as their own sections. A card owned at two rarities goes under the one with more copies. The Also check is every other rarity you own it at plus every rarity Scryfall says it was printed at on paper, so a wrong printing in the database still points at the right binder. A list writing only the front face of a double-faced card still finds it.
Liberties: Saved lists are kept on the device, not on the server, so one saved on the phone is not on the website. Keeping them on the server needs a new table, which is a schema change the request did not mention. Saving under a title that already exists replaces that list. A card you own too few of shows under its section with how many you have, and again in the missing section with how many are short.
