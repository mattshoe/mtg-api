# Autocomplete shows duplicate entries per owner, and must not

Autocomplete should never be showing ownership. But it is during the card
swap flow. It shows duplicate entries for each owner. That is unacceptable
in the new paradigm

Autocomplete is just ANY card in the world, and then later in the flow you
choose whether to add it to the collection or bring from bulk or take from
another deck
