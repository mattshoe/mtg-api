# Add Magic the Gathering lore content to the Guide

Add Magic the Gathering lore content to the Guide. 10 sections, each with the articles listed below. Every article has its own tabs.

SECTIONS AND ARTICLES

1. Brothers' War. The Thran, Koilos, The War, Argoth, Sylex Blast, Ice Age.
2. Phyrexia's Origins. Halcyon, Thran Phyrexian War, Yawgmoth's Spheres, Rath.
3. Weatherlight Saga. The Legacy, Rath, Mercadia, Invasion, Apocalypse.
4. New Phyrexia. Argentum, Memnarch, Scars, Praetors, Invasion of the Multiverse.
5. Planeswalkers. Oldwalkers, Time Rifts, The Mending, Gatewatch, Sparks Lost.
6. Nicol Bolas. Elder Dragons, Alara, Tarkir, Amonkhet, War of the Spark.
7. Ravnica. Guildpact, The Breaking, Implicit Maze, War of the Spark, Karlov Manor.
8. Eldrazi and Zendikar. The Binding, Rise, Battle for Zendikar, Emrakul on Innistrad.
9. Innistrad. Helvault, Avacyn Restored, Shadows, Eldritch Moon, Eternal Night.
10. Omenpath Era. The Omenpaths, Thunder Junction, Bloomburrow, Duskmourn, Aetherdrift, Tarkir Dragonstorm, Edge of Eternities.

Each section gets a one sentence hook.

TABS
1. Pick tabs for each article based on what matters in that story. No generic set reused across articles.
2. Order them as the story unfolds.
3. Example. The War could use Kroog, Fallaji, Phyrexian Spies, Final Battle.

WRITING
1. Narrate it like literature, not a textbook. Third person, told from outside the characters, like a historian who writes well.
2. No invented dialogue. Quote only lines that exist in official story text or card flavor text, and attribute them.
3. Show stakes and consequences through what characters did and what it cost, not through their inner thoughts.
4. Vivid but clear. Strong verbs, concrete detail, no purple prose.
5. Let each tab run as long as its story needs. End each tab on a beat that pulls into the next.
6. Audience is a Commander player who knows the cards but not the story. Work in a few real cards where they show a key moment or character, using exact card names, without breaking the narration.
7. Put the list of sets, novels, and story articles that tell each part at the end of the article, outside the narration.


ACCURACY
1. Don't invent lore. Use official Wizards story content, the official novels, and card text, checked against MTG Wiki.
2. Where a story was retconned or sources disagree, say so in one sentence and give the current official version.
3. If you can't verify something, leave it out.
4. Show sources at the end of each article.

CROSS LINKS
1. When an article mentions something covered in another article, link to it.
2. Must link to each other. Both Rath articles. Both War of the Spark articles. Emrakul on Innistrad and the Innistrad section. Invasion of the Multiverse with Sparks Lost and The Omenpaths. Tarkir with Tarkir Dragonstorm.
3. Characters who span sections, like Urza, Yawgmoth, Karn, Sorin, Nicol Bolas, Ugin, Nahiri, Elesh Norn, and Jace, get a short shared profile their names link to, listing every article they appear in.

When done, list any lore you left out because you couldn't verify it.
